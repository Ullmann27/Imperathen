# Character art studies — visual review

22 November 2026 · Issue #18 · Android-first art pipeline

## What was actually produced

Two original static characters, built in Blender 4.3.2, exported to self-contained GLB and reimported for the displayed review renders. The civilian is a youthful appearance proposal, not a decision about the campaign's canonical start age. The guard is a Roman-inspired adult role study, not a claim of archaeologically exact equipment.

## Observed visual result

`characters_studio_preview.png` is a 1280 × 960 Cycles render of the GLBs after reimport. `civilian_face_preview.png` is a 900 × 900 close-up from the same imported scene. Both were opened and visually checked. These are offline asset-review renders, not screenshots from the game or an Android device.

Role silhouettes are legible: short tunic, satchel, belt, hands and sandals for the civilian; layered curved armour, shoulder protection, cloak, scabbard and shield for the guard. Meshes use continuous loft surfaces instead of exposed ball joints. Leinen/leather/metal textures, geometry folds and vertex-colour facial features survive GLB export.

Three visual defects found in the first render were corrected and rerendered: the scalp intersected the head, eyelids/brows protruded excessively, and skin showed through the civilian's shoulder seams. The final reviewed image shows the replacement hair shell, reduced lid geometry and covered shoulder transition.

The remaining gap to the realistic target is explicit: faces, hair and garment forms still look like procedural modelling studies. They need an artistic sculpt/texturing pass, richer skin detail, controlled asymmetry, rigging, animation and deformation review before close-up protagonist use. The two roles share a face-generation basis; this does not yet constitute a broad face-variation library. No skeletal rig, movement, lip sync, collision or gameplay integration is claimed.

## Checks actually performed

- Blender generation and GLB export completed.
- Base GLBs reimported and rendered successfully.
- The static GLB validator passed all four files with no errors or warnings, including finite vertex data, unit normals, indices, embedded textures, material/UV references and scene bounds.
- Small zero-area export faces were removed at the geometry source; the validator tolerance was not relaxed.
- Palette `COLOR_0` and tangents are present after material batching.
- LOD1 files contain genuinely reduced geometry with the same placement bounds; no LOD switching performance claim is made.

## Reproduce

```sh
blender --background --python tools/art/build_characters.py -- \
  --output-dir art/visual_benchmark/assets/characters --render-preview
```

The fixed preview uses CPU Cycles without denoising because this Blender build lacks OpenImageDenoise. The generator needs the original material maps generated by the shared art pipeline. All geometry is authored in this repository; there were no third-party model downloads or paid-provider calls.
